# Bank surface

Unmodified JPEG texture maps from [Aerial Grass Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock), by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven. [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

The official `info.json` and `files.json` are included. Every downloaded 1K/2K diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness image was checked against the MD5 in that metadata. The source covers 15 m by 15 m; terrain UVs use world metres / 3 with texture repeat 0.2, preserving that scale.

Low uses a 256-pixel albedo in memory; High uses 1K detail maps; Ultra requests 2K maps on demand. Only Outer organic banks use this surface. Walkways keep their existing ground material and height.
